# Anvil Strength Club: Responsive Landing Page

**TSG Web Development, Project 02 (Beginner)** | Live site: _add Netlify/Vercel URL_

## Business concept
Anvil is a fictional coach-led strength gym in Clifton, Karachi. It targets beginners and intermediate lifters who want small classes and a written plan. The page's single job is to get visitors to book a free trial week.

## Features
- Sticky navigation, smooth scrolling, active-link highlight (IntersectionObserver)
- Hero with background image, headline, sub-headline and CTA
- Six service cards with icons and hover effects
- Three-plan pricing table with a highlighted plan
- Testimonials slider (arrows, dots, wrap-around)
- FAQ accordion (5 questions, one open at a time)
- Contact form with validation and embedded Google Map
- Footer with business info, quick links, social links
- Responsive at desktop, tablet (<=991px) and mobile (<=767px) with hamburger menu

## Tech stack
HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Bootstrap 5.3 (grid only), Google Fonts (Barlow, Barlow Condensed), Google Maps embed. Hosted on Netlify.
